Privacy Policy for Kana Trainer

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Kana Trainer is a practice app for the hiragana and katakana alphabets. This
policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: it does
not collect any of it.

What the app collects

Nothing. Kana Trainer does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. There
are no accounts, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
crash reporting.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves your practice settings and the score reports of your runs. This
data stays on your computer, in the local application data folder that Windows
gives the app. It is never uploaded anywhere.

You can export your reports to a .kt-report file and import them again, for
example to move them to another device. That file is written where you choose
to save it, and moving it somewhere else is entirely up to you.

Uninstalling the app removes it along with the data it stored.

Network use

Kana Trainer works fully offline. All characters, audio and images are part of
the installed app. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data at all, it collects
no data from children either.

Permissions

Kana Trainer asks for no special Windows permissions beyond reading and writing
its own data, plus access to a file you pick yourself when you export or import
reports.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with a new release of the app
and the date at the top is updated.
